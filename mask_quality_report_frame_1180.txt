[METRIC DEFINITIONS]

• IoU (Intersection over Union): Ratio of intersection to union of predicted and ground truth masks
• Dice Coefficient: 2 * Intersection / (Sum of masks), ranges from 0 to 1
• Precision: True Positives / (True Positives + False Positives)
• Recall: True Positives / (True Positives + False Negatives)
• F1 Score: Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall
• AP Foreground: TP / (TP + FP)
• AP Background: TN / (TN + FN)
• mAP: (AP_foreground + AP_background) / 2
